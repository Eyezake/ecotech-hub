A simple handover checklist

Before a device leaves you.

Use this as a starting point, then follow the guidance of your manufacturer, local authority, or certified collection partner.

01
Decide whether it can stay usefulRepair, upgrade, donate, sell, or pass it on if it is safe and suitable.
02
Protect your informationBack up what you need, sign out of accounts, remove SIM and memory cards, and factory-reset where appropriate.
03
Separate special itemsKeep loose batteries, bulbs, and other items that require dedicated collection out of general waste.
04
Confirm the destinationCheck accepted items, fees, drop-off instructions, and whether the provider is authorized to handle e-waste.
