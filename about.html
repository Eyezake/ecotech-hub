About EcoTech Hub

Better technology habits start with better information.

EcoTech Hub is an independent educational platform about the devices we use, the waste they create, and the choices that keep people and materials safer.

Why this exists

Electronic waste is easy to ignore because it often disappears into a drawer, a box, or a general waste stream. But a discarded phone, laptop, cable, or appliance still contains materials, energy, and personal data that deserve thoughtful handling.

We created EcoTech Hub to make the subject approachable. Our work connects the big picture—resource use, pollution, repair culture, and circular design—with the small decisions people make at home, in classrooms, and at work.

A useful rule of thumb: keep a device useful for as long as it is safe and practical, then choose a route that supports reuse or responsible material recovery.
Our promise

Clear, practical, non-judgmental guidance.

We explain the “why” and the “how” together, point to safer next steps, and encourage people to check local rules because collection options vary by place.
