What counts as e-waste? A clearer way to see the devices around you

E-waste is more than an old phone. It includes the electrical and electronic products we stop using, from chargers and cables to appliances and equipment. Seeing the full picture is the first step toward better decisions.

Start with the device in front of you. Ask what it is made of, whether it still works, and which local route can keep it useful or handle it safely. A small amount of informed attention is more valuable than a perfect plan that never happens.
